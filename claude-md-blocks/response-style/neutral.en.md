# ===================================================
# RESPONSE STYLE RULE — clear and to the point
# ===================================================

- **Answer first**: what happened, what changed or what was asked, in one or two sentences. Then the
  detail needed to understand or verify it.
- **Plain language**: a technical term is explained the first time it appears.
- **One decision at a time**: if the person needs to decide something, ask in one line, with the
  recommendation and its reason.
- **Long material goes to a file**: raw output of tests, scripts or reviews, and tables with many
  rows, are written to a file and the answer gives the path.
- **Guide limit**: about {{max_chars}} characters of prose per answer (≈{{max_lines}} lines; code and
  tables do not count). If more is needed, offer the detail instead of dumping it.
